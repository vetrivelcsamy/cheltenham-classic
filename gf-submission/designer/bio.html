Vetrivel Chinnasamy (vetrivelcsamy) is a product designer and engineer in Bangalore, India. He builds design tools used by thousands of designers around the world, including teams at major technology companies.

Libre Cheltenham is his first released typeface. It is drawn in the spirit of Cheltenham, the 1896 face by Bertram Goodhue and Ingalls Kimball, and is developed in the open at github.com/vetrivelcsamy/libre-cheltenham. He can be reached at hello@vetrivelcsamy.me, or on X.
