Vetrivel Chinnasamy is a product designer and engineer based in Bangalore, India. He is Principal Designer at Gated Society, and the founder of tomake.design, where he builds design tools used by around sixty thousand people.

Cheltenham Classic is his first released typeface. It is drawn in the spirit of Cheltenham, the 1896 face by Bertram Goodhue and Ingalls Kimball, and is developed in the open at github.com/vetrivelcsamy/cheltenham-classic.
